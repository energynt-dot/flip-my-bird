For bird brains

What is this?

Energy companies pay you to sign up, then quietly charge loyal customers more. Flip My Bird helps you do the opposite: switch every few months, pocket the bonus, and never lose track of where you're up to.

How to flip, in five steps

  1. Find a deal. Look for a sign-up bonus, ideally through a cashback site.
  2. Add it here. Put in the company, the day you were connected, and the bonuses.
  3. Sit tight. Each bonus makes you stay a while. The big number counts down to the day you're free.
  4. Chase the money. Tap Paid when a bonus lands. If one's late, the app tells you to go squawk at them.
  5. Flip. When the bird flies, pick the next deal and tap I've flipped. Repeat.

The three bonuses

Check for all three on every flip. They stack more often than you'd think.

  • Retailer bonus A credit from the energy company itself for signing up.
  • Econnex A reward for switching through the Econnex comparison site.
  • Cashback site Money from ShopBack, TopCashback or Cashrewards. They often send you through Econnex, but the cashback site is the one that pays, and it often pays more.

Each bonus has its own rule for how long you must stay. The app waits for whichever ends last, so you don't have to work it out.

Words that trip people up

  • Stay rule. How long you must stay to keep a bonus. Leave early and you may lose it.
  • Can't come back for. After you leave, how long before that company treats you as a new customer again. Often 12 months.
  • Tracked. The cashback site has seen your sign-up. Not paid yet.
  • Supply charge. What you pay per day just to be connected. With solar, it's most of your bill.
  • Feed-in. What they pay you for the solar you send out.
  • Considering. A deal you're thinking about. It doesn't count until you switch.

The birds

Every dollar you've actually received hatches you up the ladder. Your bird sleeps while you wait, wakes when you're two weeks out, and flies when you're free to flip.

The fine print

  • Your deals are yours. They're saved to your own account or your own phone. Nobody else can see them. Use Backup now and then.
  • Check the terms yourself. Offers change, and each one has its own rules. The app only knows what you type in.
  • Estimates are estimates. Cost figures come from the rates and usage you enter. Your bill is the real answer.
  • Not financial advice. Just a bird with a spreadsheet. Flip My Bird isn't connected to any energy company or cashback site.
